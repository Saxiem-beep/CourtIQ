# CourtIQ 🏆🧠
**AI Performance Coaching for Athletes & Performers**
CourtIQ is a B2B SaaS platform that democratizes elite coaching. We provide college teams, hostel sports programs, and dance academies with real-time, personalized feedback on technique, workload, and injury risk. 

## 🚀 The Problem & Solution
Most grassroots sports programs share one coach across 50-400 students. Feedback is subjective, and progress tracking is manual. CourtIQ solves this by unifying three core technologies:

1. **Coaching Knowledge Assistant (RAG):** Built on LlamaIndex and ChromaDB, athletes can ask natural-language questions and receive answers grounded in indexed coaching manuals and past session notes, completely eliminating hallucinations (Validated at 92% accuracy).
2. **Performance & Risk Prediction (ML):** A SMOTE-balanced classical ML pipeline that scores injury risk and predicts performance trajectories from training-load data, passing strict demographic fairness audits. 
3. **Real-Time Leaderboards:** A high-performance C++ engine leveraging PBDS and order-statistic trees to deliver instant, O(log n) rank updates for massive player pools.

## 📈 Business Model & Traction
* **Market:** $1.2B+ Target Market (India, Y1-3).
* **Pricing:** B2B SaaS charging $3-$6/athlete/month for institutions.
* **Status:** Prototypes validated. Currently executing Phase 1 (Months 1-4): Piloting the MVP with 2 hostel/college teams (basketball + dance).
